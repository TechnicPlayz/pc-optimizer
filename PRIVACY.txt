VELORA PC - PRIVACY

- Velora PC collects no telemetry and no personal data.
- Nothing about your PC is uploaded anywhere.
- Your settings, history and logs are stored only on your PC, in
  %LocalAppData%\VeloraPC.
- The only network request is the optional update check, which asks GitHub for
  the latest release number. You can turn it off in Settings > Updates.
- Uninstalling removes the app, its scheduled task, its startup entry and its
  local data.
