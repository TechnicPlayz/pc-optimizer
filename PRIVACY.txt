VELORA PC - PRIVACY NOTICE (TEMPLATE)

*** Template: review before commercial distribution. ***

- Velora PC collects no telemetry and no personal data.
- Nothing about your PC is uploaded.
- Settings, history and logs are stored only on your PC in %LocalAppData%\VeloraPC.
- The only network request is the optional update check, which asks GitHub for the latest release number. You can turn it off in Settings > Updates.
- Uninstalling removes the app, its scheduled task, its startup entry and its local data.
